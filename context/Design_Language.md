## Design Principles

This website must **never** look AI-generated.

Follow these principles throughout the implementation:

- Design for clarity over decoration.
- Use clean layouts with generous whitespace.
- Prefer sharp edges (`rounded-md` / `rounded-lg`) over overly rounded cards.
- Use purple glow sparingly as an accent, not as the primary design element.
- Keep typography minimal, modern, and readable.
- Write concise, human copy. Avoid generic AI marketing language.
- Avoid unnecessary gradients, floating blobs, oversized glassmorphism, or excessive animations.
- Use subtle Framer Motion transitions (fade, slight lift, opacity) that support the experience rather than distract from it.
- The event poster is the primary visual identity—build the interface around it instead of competing with it.
- Every section should have a clear purpose. Remove anything that feels decorative but doesn't improve usability.
- The final result should feel like a handcrafted product microsite designed by an experienced frontend developer, not an AI-generated landing page.
